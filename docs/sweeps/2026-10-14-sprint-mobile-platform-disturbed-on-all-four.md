# Sprint lane, disturbed: mobile platform

Each disturbance tried on the same 9 set-ups - the design as designed and 8 drawn at random within the ranges of ADR 0024 - driven straight ahead at full speed for 20 s.

| Disturbance | Every goal met | Drawn at random | Broken by it | Last goal, median change | Pushed | Went back | Missed in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| none | 9 of 9 | 8 of 8, at least 69 % | - | - | - | - | - |
| shoved 4 N s after the start | 9 of 9 | 8 of 8, at least 69 % | 0 of 8, at most 31 % | +0.1 s | 16.3 cm (6.2 to 23.6) | - | - |
| shoved 8 N s after the start | 3 of 9 | 2 of 8, at least 5 % | 6 of 8, 35 to 97 %; p = 0.0312 | +0.4 s | 89.1 cm (50.9 to 136.5) | - | repeat 2, repeat 3, repeat 4, repeat 5, repeat 6, repeat 7 |

What each disturbance does:

- **shoved 4 N s after the start**: 4 N s over 0.1 s, 90 deg left of its heading, after "start", 1 s later.
- **shoved 8 N s after the start**: 8 N s over 0.1 s, 90 deg left of its heading, after "start", 1 s later.

A run counts when it meets every goal (start line, 5 m finish, stayed upright). "Last goal, median change" compares each set-up with itself undisturbed. "Pushed" is how far the machine's base went the way it was pushed, a second after the push; "Went back", in how many set-ups the program drove back to a stop it had been pushed off (ADR 0031). "Broken by it" pairs each drawn set-up with itself undisturbed - the two are one run until the event, step for step (ADR 0036) - and counts those that met every goal undisturbed and missed one with it, with the share of successes it breaks (95 % confidence); "rescued" counts the set-ups it turned the other way, and p is the exact McNemar test on the set-ups whose outcomes differ. The rate is over the set-ups drawn at random (ADR 0028); the design as designed is a chosen run.
