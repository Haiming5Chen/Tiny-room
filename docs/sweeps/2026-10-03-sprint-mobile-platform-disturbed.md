# Sprint lane, disturbed: mobile platform

Each disturbance tried on the same 9 set-ups - the design as designed and 8 drawn at random within the ranges of ADR 0024 - driven straight ahead at full speed for 20 s.

| Disturbance | Every goal met | Drawn at random | Last goal, median change | Pushed | Missed in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| none | 9 of 9 | 8 of 8, at least 69 % | - | - | - |
| shoved 4 N s after the start | 9 of 9 | 8 of 8, at least 69 % | +0.1 s | 12.4 cm (7.7 to 21.5) | - |
| shoved 8 N s after the start | 2 of 9 | 2 of 8, at least 5 % | +0.8 s | 78.8 cm (46.6 to 121.4) | as designed, repeat 2, repeat 3, repeat 4, repeat 5, repeat 6, repeat 7 |

What each disturbance does:

- **shoved 4 N s after the start**: 4 N s over 0.1 s, 90 deg left of its heading, after "start", 1 s later.
- **shoved 8 N s after the start**: 8 N s over 0.1 s, 90 deg left of its heading, after "start", 1 s later.

A run counts when it meets every goal (start line, 5 m finish, stayed upright). "Last goal, median change" compares each set-up with itself undisturbed. "Pushed" is how far the machine's base went the way it was pushed, a second after the push. The rate is over the set-ups drawn at random (ADR 0028); the design as designed is a chosen run.
